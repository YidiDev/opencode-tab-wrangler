# Community code of conduct

We want a welcoming, respectful project where people can report problems without being blamed.

- Critique code and ideas constructively, not people.
- Respect different experiences, identities, abilities, and communication styles.
- Accept corrections and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Protect others' privacy. Never post credentials, transcripts, or identifying information without permission.

Harassment, discriminatory insults, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and persistent disruption are unacceptable. This applies to issues, PRs, reviews, and other project spaces, including interactions while representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

[@YidiDev](https://github.com/YidiDev) is the responsible maintainer. To arrange a private report, open a minimal issue requesting a private conduct-reporting channel, without naming participants or including incident details. Use GitHub's **Report abuse** controls for violations of GitHub's policies. Do not publish sensitive incident details.

The maintainer may remove inappropriate content, request corrections, warn participants, or restrict participation, considering severity and recurrence. Reports are handled as confidentially as practical; no response time is guaranteed. Retaliation against good-faith reporting is unacceptable.
